Math Rotor 3D

Roll into the right answer.

Steer a ball down a neon tunnel. Every ring asks a math question; slide left or right to roll through the right answer. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard, practise the skills you want, and on iPhone and iPad, climb the Game Center leaderboards. Available for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Help

How do I steer?

Slide your finger left or right anywhere on the screen. The slider at the bottom shows where the ball is heading.

I bought No Ads on another device

Tap the restore button (the circular arrow at the top left of the menu), signed in with the same Apple Account or Google Account you used to buy it.

The leaderboard won’t open

Leaderboards use Apple’s Game Center, so they’re on iPhone and iPad only. Sign in to Game Center in Settings › Game Center, then try again.

How do I change my ad choice?

Tap ? on the game’s menu, then Credits, then Ad choices. See the privacy policy for what that changes.
