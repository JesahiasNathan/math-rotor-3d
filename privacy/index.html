Privacy Policy

Math Rotor 3D · Last updated October 17, 2026

Math Rotor 3D is a math game for iPhone, iPad and Android made by Jesahias Rodriguez (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is used when you play, who uses it and why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details, and we don’t run servers that receive information about you.
  • Your scores, progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Ads keep the game free. Our ad partner, Unity, collects some information about your device to show and measure ads. Whether those ads are personalised is your choice, and you can change it at any time.
  • Leaderboards (on iPhone and iPad) and the No Ads purchase are handled by Apple or Google.

1. Information kept on your device

To remember your progress, the game stores the following on your phone or tablet:

  • your best scores for each difficulty;
  • coins and gems, the balls and tunnels you’ve unlocked, and the ones you’ve picked;
  • your difficulty, practice, sound, music and steering settings;
  • when you last collected the daily reward;
  • your answer about personalised ads, whether you’ve rated the game, and whether you’ve bought No Ads.

This information never leaves your device and we never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Advertising

Math Rotor 3D shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, Unity’s ad mediation service, which fills them from the Unity Ads and ironSource Ads networks. All three are run by Unity Technologies (“Unity”). To show ads, Unity’s software in the game collects:

  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system, language, time zone, screen size, network type and app version;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location (such as country or city) worked out from it;
  • device identifiers: on iPhone and iPad, Apple’s identifier for vendors (IDFV), and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking; on Android, the Android advertising ID and app set ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings;
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown, watched to the end or tapped, and any resulting installs;
  • technical information about ad loading problems and crashes in the ad software.

Unity uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and, only with your permission, show ads that suit your interests. Unity explains how it uses and shares this information, and the rights you have, in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Buying No Ads removes the ads that appear between games. Ads you choose to watch for a reward, such as a second chance, stay available.

3. Your ad choices

  • When you first open the game, it asks whether ads may be personalised. On iPhone and iPad, iOS then asks whether the app may track you, and ads are personalised only if you say yes to both; on Android, only if you say yes. If you don’t, you still see ads, but they aren’t based on your interests or activity in other apps.
  • To change your answer at any time, tap ? on the game’s menu, then Credits, then Ad choices.
  • To turn tracking off for this or every app: on iPhone and iPad, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads) and delete your advertising ID.

We pass your answer to Unity as your consent (or refusal) under the GDPR and UK GDPR, and as an opt-out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

4. Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you’re signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple so they can appear on the leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. Apple handles your Game Center account and data under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app.

5. Purchases

No Ads is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details, Apple Account or Google Account. The game only learns that the purchase was made, so it can turn the ads off and restore the purchase on your other devices.

6. Children

Math Rotor 3D is made for a general audience. It isn’t directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child’s information has been collected through the game, contact us and we’ll help have it deleted.

7. Keeping and protecting information

Because we don’t receive information about you, we don’t store any. Information on your device stays until you delete the app. Unity keeps advertising information for the periods described in its privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to its use, or to withdraw consent. For information collected for advertising, Unity is the company that holds it; you can make requests through its privacy policy. You can also write to us and we’ll help.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s update notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: creator@structor.tech
